# Rimworld Remade

A RimWorld-inspired colony game in a 2.5D overhead 3D world (Godot 4.7, C#). Everything you see and hear — terrain,
trees, deer, colonists, the planet, the sounds of wind, rain, birds and water — is generated by the game itself.

This first version is about one thing done well: **land on a living planet, take control of a colonist, find a bow,
hunt a deer and carry the meat home.**

## Play

```
Play.bat                 builds and starts the game (main menu)
Play.bat --windowed      in a window
Play.bat --play          skip the menus: quick start on a default world
```

**New game:** Play → Select scenario (prepared, empty for now) → Create colonists (randomize anyone) → Generate world →
choose a landing site on the globe (hover to see the hexagonal regions, click to select; only temperate forests can be
settled in this version) → Next → choose a map size (300 up to 1500 cells, RimWorld's largest is 325) → Play.

**Load game** lists your saves. Save from the in-game menu (Esc).

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Camera | W A S D / arrows to pan (Z Q S D on AZERTY), mouse wheel to zoom, middle mouse to drag, Ctrl + middle mouse or [ ] to rotate |
| Select | Left click a colonist (or a card in the colonist bar; double-click to focus) |
| Orders | Right click: menu of everything the selected colonist can do there (go, pick up, eat, gather berries, drink, open/close door, hunt, attack) |
| Draft | R — drafted colonists stand by and only follow your orders |
| **Direct control** | C or the *Direct control* button under the colonist panel. Only one colonist at a time |
| — move | W A S D (rebindable), Shift to sprint |
| — interact | E: pick up items, gather berries, drink, open/close doors. With several things in reach, roll the mouse wheel to choose (they are highlighted and listed) |
| — action menu | quick right click on a thing |
| — aim / shoot | hold the right mouse button to aim (a red line shows your reach: bow range or melee reach, with the distance), left click to shoot or swing. You can move while aiming |
| Time | Space pause, 1 2 3 4 speeds (Normal, Fast, Faster, Ultra) |
| Panels | Bio, Equipment, Needs, Health tabs above the colonist panel. F2 planet view. Tab next colonist. F focus |

All keys can be rebound in **Options → Controls**, which also has a one-click **AZERTY (ZQSD)** remap (detected
automatically on French/Belgian keyboard layouts).

## The colonist panel

- **Bio** — full name, sex, biological age and chronological age in brackets when they differ (cryptosleep), traits,
  12 skills from 0 to 20 with passions (skills have no effect yet).
- **Equipment** — a body figure with layer tabs (Skin, Middle, Outer, Headgear, Eyes, Belt) showing what is worn where
  and what is free; the weapon in hand; and a grid inventory per container (trouser pockets, jacket pockets, satchel).
  Drag items to rearrange (right click while dragging rotates), right click for Equip / Eat / Drop. The load bar shows
  the carried mass against your body mass: up to 25 % comfortable, up to 45 % encumbered (a soldier's approach-march
  load), up to 70 % heavily encumbered, beyond that barely moving.
- **Needs** — food, thirst and sleep, decaying at real-life rates (thirst faster in the heat). No effects yet.
- **Health** — the body figure: click a part to see its organs and bones (RimWorld's anatomy) with their condition.
  *Operations* → *Add bill* (empty for now).

## The living planet

The globe is the same simulation as your map. Each region has a natural temperature, precipitation and soil moisture
that change with the seasons, latitude, altitude and travelling weather systems. Your map's outdoor temperature is
its region's value plus the day/night cycle; its rain, snow, fog, wind and wet or snowy ground come from the same
place. Open the planet (F2) during play to see the live temperature, elevation and precipitation maps.

## Graphics options

Options → Graphics: fullscreen, vsync, render scale, shadow quality, ambient occlusion, volumetric fog, grass density,
frame-rate limit. Lower render scale, shadows Medium/Low and grass density are the most effective on slower machines.

## Files

- Saves and settings: `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Rimworld Remade\`
- Logs (when run from the project folder): `logs\` — please attach the newest `game_*.log` when reporting a problem.
